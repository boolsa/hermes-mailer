Hermes Mailer

A private, single-user automation tool.

Hermes Mailer is a personal assistant tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uses Google APIs to send scheduled emails (such as daily news digests and study lessons) from its owner's Google account, and to read replies to those emails so the owner can give feedback.

It is not a public service. It is not offered, sold, or made available to anyone else, and the only Google account it ever connects to is its owner's.
