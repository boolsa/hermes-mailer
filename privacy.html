Privacy Policy

Hermes Mailer · Last updated September 27, 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes Mailer is a private tool with exactly one user: its owner. It only ever connects to its owner's own Google account. No other person's data is collected.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) to send the owner's scheduled emails, read replies to those emails, and help the owner with their own documents and calendar.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform tasks the owner has asked the app to do.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • To write the owner's emails, content may be processed by AI model services the owner has chosen, solely to carry out the owner's own tasks.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes Mailer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/boolsa/hermes-mailer.
